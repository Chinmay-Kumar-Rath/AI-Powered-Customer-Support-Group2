---
title: Sensitive data sent to the wrong recipient
category: Security
keywords: security, data exposure, email
---
Question: What should I do about sensitive data sent to the wrong recipient?

Suggested answer: Follow the safe first-line checks below and use only organization-approved tools and procedures.

Steps:
1. Do not forward the message or attempt an unauthorized recall.
2. Record the recipients, data type, time, and delivery method.
3. Notify the security or privacy team immediately.
4. Follow instructions about recipient contact and evidence preservation.
5. Do not delete records unless authorized.

Escalate if: the issue continues, affects multiple users, or involves suspected compromise, possible data loss, or an unsafe condition.

Safety note: These are general first-line checks. Do not open equipment, alter firmware, change corporate network or security settings, bypass MFA, disable endpoint protection, or modify production data without authorization.
