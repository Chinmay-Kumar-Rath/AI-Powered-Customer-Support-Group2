Question: What should I do about password rejected by policy?

Suggested answer: Follow the safe first-line checks below and use only organization-approved tools and procedures.

Steps:
1. Read the approved password policy requirements.
2. Choose a new password that does not reuse an old one.
3. Avoid personal information and shared examples.
4. Submit the change through the approved portal.
5. Contact support without sending the password.

Escalate if: the issue continues, affects multiple users, or involves suspected compromise, possible data loss, or an unsafe condition.

Safety note: These are general first-line checks. Do not open equipment, alter firmware, change corporate network or security settings, bypass MFA, disable endpoint protection, or modify production data without authorization.
